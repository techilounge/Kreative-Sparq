# Generation Prompt Set

The images were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. The supplied homepage concept was used as a style and mood reference only.

## Shared direction

- Premium African business and creative editorial photography
- Modern Nigerian visual context
- Dark forest, terracotta, charcoal, mineral white, muted sage, and restrained gold
- Natural skin and material texture
- Confident, thoughtful, contemporary mood
- No logos, client marks, readable text, watermarks, metrics, visual damage, glitch effects, or burnt textures
- All depicted people are fictional editorial subjects
